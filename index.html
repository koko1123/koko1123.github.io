A pure-Zig Ethereum library: ABI, RLP, secp256k1, Keccak-256, HD wallets, EIP-712, JSON-RPC, WebSocket, ENS, and ERC-20/721, with function selectors and event topics computed at compile time.

Benchmarked against alloy.rs, Rust’s leading Ethereum library, with the harness and the five losses published: RLP EIP-1559 encode 24x, ABI dynamic decode 8.4x, secp256k1 sign and recover 4.2x.
